# Open data and libraries

- KanjiVG, Ulrich Apel and contributors: https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/ — CC BY-SA 3.0. Paths for 産, 栽 and 旬 were extracted from the original SVGs into `public/data/strokes.json`. Original source: https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg/tree/master/kanji. Adapted stroke data remains CC BY-SA 3.0. License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- JMdict, Electronic Dictionary Research and Development Group: https://www.edrdg.org/ — CC BY-SA 4.0. Russian and English glosses distributed through https://github.com/scriptin/jmdict-simplified, release 3.6.2+20260907165411. Entries were reduced to spelling, reading, language and glosses in `public/data/dictionary.json`. Adapted dictionary remains CC BY-SA 4.0. License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
- TS-FSRS: https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/ts-fsrs — MIT. Review engine configured for 90% target retention with short learning steps.

Dictionary glosses provide possible meanings, not a guaranteed translation in context. Variant generation covers common endings and is not a full morphological analyzer.
